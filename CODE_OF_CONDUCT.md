# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the Leraniode community, we pledge to make participation a **positive, inclusive, and harassment-free experience** for everyone, regardless of skill, experience, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or background.

We pledge to:

- Treat everyone with **respect**.
- Actively foster a **friendly and welcoming environment**.
- Address conflicts constructively and professionally.
- Refrain from **discriminatory, abusive, or offensive behavior**.

## Our Standards

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or exclusion.
- Use of offensive language or imagery.
- Threats of violence.
- Disrespecting differing opinions or constructive feedback.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the org maintainers:

**Email:** dominionthedeveloper@gmail.com

All complaints will be reviewed and responded to **promptly and confidentially**.

## Enforcement Actions

Depending on severity, enforcement actions may include:

- Warnings
- Temporary or permanent bans from contributions or community spaces
- Removal of inappropriate content
